Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information WebGeek collects when you use this website, why we collect it, how we look after it and the rights you have under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018).

Who we are

WebGeek is a web design and development agency based at Unit 8, St Gabriel's Business Park, Bristol BS5 0RT, United Kingdom. We are the "controller" of the personal information described in this policy. If you have any questions, email us at info@webgeek.uk.

What we collect

When you send us an enquiry through our contact form, we collect:

  • your name and work email address
  • your company name, if you give it
  • the type of project you're interested in and any details you share in your message

If you email us directly, we collect your email address and anything you include in your message.

Like most websites, our hosting and security providers automatically record technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you request. This is used to keep the website secure and running properly.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and, if you wish, to arrange a discovery call and prepare a quote. Our lawful basis is taking steps at your request before entering into a contract, and our legitimate interest in responding to people who contact us.
  • To keep the website secure, including detecting spam and abuse. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in protecting our website and visitors.

We don't sell your information, and we won't add you to a marketing mailing list without your permission.

Who we share it with

We only share your information with trusted providers who help us run this website:

  • Formspree, which receives contact form messages and delivers them to our inbox.
  • GoDaddy and Sucuri, which host and protect this website.
  • Google Fonts, which supplies the fonts on this website. Your browser connects to Google's servers to load them, which shares your IP address with Google.

Some of these providers may process information outside the UK, including in the United States. Where this happens, we rely on appropriate safeguards recognised under UK law, such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or the UK–US data bridge.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for up to 24 months so we can refer back to our conversations, unless you become a client, in which case we keep project records for as long as needed for the work and for legal and accounting purposes (usually 6 years). You can ask us to delete your enquiry at any time.

Cookies and storage

This website doesn't use advertising or analytics cookies. After you send an enquiry, your browser stores a small note of the time it was sent, so we can prevent duplicate submissions within a minute. It contains no personal information and stays on your device.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • ask us to correct information that is wrong or incomplete
  • ask us to delete your information
  • object to or ask us to restrict how we use your information
  • ask us to transfer your information to another organisation

To use any of these rights, email info@webgeek.uk. We'll respond within one month.

Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please contact us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
