(05) FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can't see yours? Email info@webgeek.uk.

How much does a website cost?+

Every project is quoted individually. The price depends on the number of pages, how much content we create, and whether you need e-commerce or integrations. After a free discovery call you get a written scope and a fixed quote.

How long does a project take?+

Most websites launch in 3–8 weeks depending on size. Online stores usually take 6–10 weeks, and web applications are delivered in phases.

Do you only work with businesses in Bristol?+

No. We're based in Bristol and work with businesses across the UK, in person or over video calls.

Can we update the website ourselves?+

Yes. Every site comes with an easy-to-use CMS, a training session and written guides.

Do you work with existing designs or brands?+

Yes. We can build from your existing brand guidelines or designs, or create a new identity from scratch.

What happens after launch?+

You get 30 days of free support, then can move onto a care plan for hosting, maintenance and continuous improvement.
