05 — FAQ

Good questions.

How much does a website cost in the UK?+

In the UK, a small-business website typically costs £800–£3,000 from a freelancer and £6,000–£20,000+ from an agency. WebGeek's fixed-price packages start at £1,495 for up to 5 pages, £3,950 for a 12-page lead-generation site and £5,950 for an online store, excluding VAT.

Why are you more affordable than London agencies?+

We're a lean, remote-first senior team. There's no city-centre office, no account managers and no layers of juniors to pay for. The money goes into design and code, not overheads.

Is the price really fixed?+

Yes. After the discovery call you get a written scope and a fixed quote. The price only changes if you ask for something new, and we always agree that with you in writing first.

How do payments work?+

You pay 50% to start and 50% at launch. You can also spread the cost over 3 interest-free monthly payments. All prices exclude VAT.

How long does a typical project take?+

Launch sites go live in 2–3 weeks, Growth sites in 4–6 weeks and online stores in 6–8 weeks. Web applications are scoped in phases, with a first release usually within 12 weeks.

Can we update the site ourselves?+

Yes. Every build ships with a headless CMS or Webflow editor, plus a recorded training session and written docs.

Do you work with existing designs?+

Absolutely — we regularly build from in-house or partner designs and can tighten them up for performance and accessibility.

What happens after launch?+

You get 30 days of free support, then can move onto a care plan for hosting, maintenance and continuous improvement.
